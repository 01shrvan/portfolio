---
title: why i'm even doing this
tldr: "starting a blog in 2026. no plan, no schedule. i keep losing ideas in my notes app and writing them down is the only thing that makes me finish a thought."
date: 2026-01-04
---

so i'm starting a blog. in 2026. feels a bit late.

the actual reason is boring. i get an idea, think about it for a week, maybe open a figma file or start a repo, and then it just stops. it doesn't die because it was bad. it dies because it never left my head, and nothing in my head has to be finished.

writing does. you can't write a paragraph about something you only half understand. you get two sentences in and the gap shows up. that's the whole appeal, honestly.

i've been waiting until i had something worth saying before saying anything, which is backwards. the people i actually learn from post the middle of the work, not the end of it.

so this will mostly be the middle. things i built and what broke, calls i'm still not sure about, and the occasional thing i got wrong and only noticed months later.

no schedule. no niche. i'll probably go quiet for a while at some point.

if you're reading this in a year and there are three posts total, then that's what happened. i mostly just need somewhere to finish a thought.
