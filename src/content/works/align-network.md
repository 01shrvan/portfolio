the premise is that social networks reward virality rather than thinking, so posts surface
by how well they align with what you actually care about instead of by follower count.

that is also the hard part. matching people by how they think is a much fuzzier problem
than matching them by who they already follow, and 30 active users is not enough signal to
know whether the matching is any good yet.
