*parchi* is the word people already use for the slip a doctor hands them, so the product
is named after the thing it organises in the language of the people who own the folder.

the hard part is not storage, it is reading the page correctly. a scanned pdf with no
text layer, a two-column lab table that interleaves into nonsense, a value that loses its
unit and its reference range. a paper folder is unhelpful but it never states a wrong
haemoglobin with confidence, so anything built here has to declare what it could not read
rather than quietly filling the gap.
